Our Story

Welcome to Angelo's
Ristorante Italiano

We use the freshest ingredients available to prepare your meal. Good cuisine cannot be rushed — every sauce is homemade, every dish prepared with care and the tradition of authentic Italian cooking.

From our signature NY-style thin crust pizza to our chef's recommended seafood specialties, Angelo's brings the flavors of Italy to Holiday, Florida. Whether you're joining us for a family dinner, a casual lunch, or ordering takeout, we promise an experience that keeps you coming back.

— Angelo Rossi, Chef & Owner
